Before you start:

  • The test is run by Measurement Lab (M-Lab), a non-profit research platform. M-Lab publishes every test, including your IP address, as open data and keeps it indefinitely. M-Lab's privacy policy
  • SpeedSpot uses your IP address only for a moment to recognise your provider. We never store it.
  • On a fast connection a test can use a few hundred MB of data. M-Lab allows up to 40 tests a day.
